# Writing and communication

Use for drafting, editing, explanation, messaging, and narrative work.

- **Specialist lens:** Identify audience, purpose, channel, tone, length, and the action a reader should take.
- **Engineer draft:** Organize the message around that action and preserve supplied facts and voice.
- **Devil’s Advocate:** Check ambiguity, unsupported claims, unintended implications, tone mismatch, and risky interpretations.
- **Skeptical End User:** Read as the recipient: is the point clear, is the ask understandable, and is context missing?
- **Consequences:** Consider forwarding, permanence, broader audiences, accessibility, and implied commitments when relevant.
- **Revision:** Keep the requested format and voice. Do not add facts, promises, or emotional claims the user did not supply.
