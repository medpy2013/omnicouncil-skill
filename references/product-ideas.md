# Product and idea evaluation

Use for evaluating an idea, product, feature, business concept, or roadmap choice.

- **Specialist lens:** Define the user, problem, current alternative, evidence of demand, and outcome that would validate the idea.
- **Engineer draft:** Form a testable proposition and a low-cost experiment with a clear success/failure threshold.
- **Devil’s Advocate:** Challenge selection bias, willingness-to-pay assumptions, distribution, defensibility, operational cost, and distorted feedback.
- **Skeptical End User:** Ask why someone would switch, what feels confusing or costly, and what would cause abandonment.
- **Consequences:** Consider support burden, cannibalization, network effects, trust, regulation, and scale effects when relevant.
- **Revision:** Separate evidence from hypothesis. Prefer reversible experiments when uncertainty is high.
