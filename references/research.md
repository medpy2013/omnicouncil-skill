# Research and evidence

Use for factual investigation, synthesis, comparisons, and research-backed recommendations.

- **Specialist lens:** Translate the question into answerable claims; identify the necessary source quality, recency, and scope.
- **Engineer draft:** Set a search/collection plan and synthesis structure. Use available research tools when appropriate.
- **Devil’s Advocate:** Look for contrary evidence, selection effects, stale data, ambiguous terms, and sources that repeat rather than independently corroborate.
- **Skeptical End User:** Check whether the conclusion answers the actual question and makes uncertainty clear.
- **Consequences:** Consider how incomplete or biased evidence could change the recommendation or how it might be misapplied.
- **Revision:** Cite sources near supported claims. Distinguish sourced facts, inference, and open questions. Never invent citations or claim research that did not occur.
