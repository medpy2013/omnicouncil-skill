# General problem-solving

Use only when no specialized reference is a better fit.

- **Specialist lens:** Define the desired outcome, constraints, decision owner, and whether choices are reversible.
- **Engineer draft:** Propose a practical next step or solution with enough detail to act.
- **Devil’s Advocate:** Test the most consequential assumption and what could realistically make the plan fail; report no material flaw when appropriate.
- **Skeptical End User:** Check clarity, effort, accessibility, and likely misinterpretation.
- **Consequences:** Consider affected people, recurring cost, incentives, and effects of success when material.
- **Revision:** Make the result usable, acknowledge uncertainty, and avoid process for process’s sake.
